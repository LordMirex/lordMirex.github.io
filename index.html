About Me / Journey

My software journey started in 2021 while preparing for JAMB examinations. I began by exploring how websites work with basic HTML and was initially driven by curiosity about web monetization concepts like AdSense and AdWords.

That curiosity quickly grew into a deeper commitment to full web development. During my Production Engineering studies at the University of Benin, I continued building technical skills and eventually earned a web development scholarship in 2024. The program covered PHP, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, jQuery, and AJAX.

After training, I worked on client solutions and Laravel-based applications, with a strong focus on backend logic, API development, and deployment workflows. As of 2026, I am in my final year (400 level), completing industrial attachment and actively engineering practical backend systems, SaaS products, and scalable server-side tools.

Today, I focus primarily on PHP/Laravel backend development, Linux VPS deployments, SaaS architecture, and robust API services.
